FIELD MANUAL / FLIGHT & RESCUE

People first. Always.

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Release to slow into a hover. Descend vertically for a safe landing; touching down at speed can destroy your helicopter.

X cycles west → forward → east → forward. Your facing is independent of your movement. Hold Space to shoot in the direction you face. In the forward position, Space fires down at tanks and barracks.

Land beside a waving group and stay still. Hostages board automatically, up to 16. Land on the marked home pad to unload. Shooting, bombing or landing on hostages kills them. Passengers are lost if their helicopter is destroyed.

Tanks threaten low flight. Fighters arrive after your first delivery; homing mines join after your second. The border keeps tanks and fighters out, but mines can follow you home.

You have three helicopters. The mission ends when all 64 people are accounted for or the fleet is lost. P / Escape pauses, M toggles sound. The rescue record is saved on this device.
